Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 echel.in — Files deleted instantly · The shop owner's own logs
Echel is built around a simple promise: a customer’s document should exist only long enough to print, and nothing longer. This policy explains exactly what echel.in collects, how long a print file survives, and what a shop owner can see. We do not sell data to anyone, and we do not keep a library of your customers’ documents.
In plain languageThe most important thing: the customer's file is deleted the moment it's printed — nothing is left on the server. The shop owner can see in the admin panel logs that the job printed and the file was removed. We never store card details or UPI PINs; those go to Razorpay.

Print files are deleted immediately after printing

This is the core of how Echel works. When a customer scans a shop’s QR code on echel.in and uploads a document, that file is held only until the print-relay agent has sent it to the shop’s printer. As soon as the job is printed, the file is deleted right away — it is not archived, not backed up, and not kept for later viewing by us or by the shop.

A job that is never paid for, or never printed, is cleared automatically as well, so nothing lingers on echel.in indefinitely. Because deletion is immediate, a printed file cannot be recovered or reprinted from the server — if a customer needs another copy, they must upload it again. Customers should always keep their own copy of any document they send.

What the shop owner can see in their logs

Every shop has its own admin panel on echel.in with a print log. That log records what happened, not the document itself: job ID and time, number of pages and copies, colour or black-and-white, the amount paid and payment status, and whether the file was printed and cleared.

The shop owner can therefore verify their earnings and confirm that each customer file was removed after printing — while the document content itself is already gone. Only that shop can see its own log; one shop can never see another shop’s jobs or customers.

Information we collect

Beyond the print file itself, echel.in collects only what the service needs to work:

  • Shop account — shop name, owner name, mobile number, email, shop area, Shop ID, and password stored in hashed form;
  • Print job record — page count, copies, colour setting, paper size, amount, payment status and time;
  • Customer mobile number, where entered, so the right job can be identified at the counter;
  • Payment reference — transaction ID, amount and success or failure, received from the payment gateway;
  • Printer list from the shop computer — the names of installed printers, so you can choose which printer handles which job;
  • Basic technical logs — device type, browser, agent version, error messages and timestamps.

We do not read, index, or analyse the contents of customer documents, and we never use them for advertising, profiling, or training any model.

Payment details are never stored with us

Online payments on echel.in are handled by Razorpay or the shop’s own UPI. Card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN and net-banking passwords are entered on the payment provider’s own secure screen. Echel never sees or stores them. We receive only the transaction ID, amount, and whether it succeeded — which is what releases the print job.

Who else sees anything

We do not sell or rent personal information. Limited data is shared only where the service requires it:

  • The shop you sent the job to — which receives the print job and the counter details needed to hand it over;
  • Razorpay — to take and verify payment;
  • Our hosting and database providers — to run echel.in;
  • Authorities — only where a valid legal requirement applies.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect echel.in, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, admin access controls, and shop-level separation so no shop can reach another shop’s data. Immediate deletion of print files is itself a security measure — data that no longer exists cannot leak.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute safety. Shop owners must protect their own Shop ID, password, and the computer running the print-relay agent.

Cookies and local storage

echel.in uses cookies and browser local storage to keep a shop owner signed in to the admin panel, remember the language chosen on the homepage, and record basic errors so faults can be fixed. These are functional. You can clear or block them in your browser, though the admin panel may then not stay logged in.

Your rights

You may, subject to Indian law:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about your shop account;
  • Ask us to correct anything wrong;
  • Ask us to delete your shop account and its details, where we are not required to keep them;
  • Ask us to stop sending non-essential messages.

Send any such request through the Business Inquiry form on echel.in or the contact details in the footer.

Children

Echel is meant for shop owners and their walk-in customers. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child’s information has reached us, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the way echel.in handles data changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Anything that materially affects shops will also be shown in the admin panel.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Echel?

Echel is print-shop automation software for cyber cafes and print shops in India. The customer scans the shop's QR code, uploads a document from their phone, pays, and the shop's printer prints it automatically — with no WhatsApp, no pen drive and no file transfer.

How does Echel work, step by step?

In five steps: (1) the shop displays its QR code, (2) the customer scans it and opens the link in any phone browser, (3) the customer uploads a PDF or photo and picks black & white or colour, pages and copies, (4) the customer pays online or chooses pay-at-counter, (5) the shop's printer prints the job automatically.

Does the customer need to install an app?

No. The customer needs only a phone camera and a browser. There is nothing to download, no sign-up and no account.

Which printers are supported?

Any printer that is already installed on the shop's Windows PC — HP, Canon, Epson, Brother and others, inkjet or laser. A Wi-Fi printer is not required, because printing is done by the shop's PC, not by the phone.

Do I need a Wi-Fi printer or a special machine?

No. If your existing printer prints from your Windows PC today, it will work with Echel. You only install a small print agent on that PC.

How much does Echel cost?

Start with the free demo. Current plan prices, included features and billing cycles are shown on the Pricing section. Echel does not charge a commission on individual prints.

Who receives the customer's payment?

The shop owner receives it directly. Online payments go straight into the shop's own Razorpay or Cashfree account — Echel does not hold or route the money and takes no cut per print.

Can customers still pay cash at the counter?

Yes. Each shop can allow online payment, pay-at-counter cash, or both. The shop decides.

Why is this better than receiving files on WhatsApp?

Because your personal number stays private, files do not get lost in chat, and printing does not need manual downloading. On WhatsApp you must find the file, open it and print it yourself; here the paid job prints automatically.

How long does setup take?

About two minutes to register the shop, plus a one-time install of the Windows print agent. After that the shop only needs to display its QR code.

Is it safe? What happens to the customer's file?

Files are stored temporarily only to complete the print job and are removed afterwards. Each shop sees only its own jobs. The full retention details are on the Privacy Policy page.

What happens if the internet goes off during a job?

Nothing is lost. Jobs stay safely in the queue, and when the connection returns the print agent picks them up and prints them automatically.

Can it print passport-size photos and resumes?

Yes. Echel includes a 4×6 passport-photo layout tool and a resume maker, along with the normal document printing.

Who is Echel for?

Cyber cafes, internet cafes, stationery and photocopy shops, DTP centres and print shops in India — any small shop that prints documents for walk-in customers.

Is Echel available across India?

Yes. It works anywhere in India with a Windows PC, a printer and an internet connection. It is built and operated from India.

THE CONNECTION BEHIND THE COUNTER

Your computer.
Your printers.

Download the latest Echel software from QR & Downloads and install it on the Windows computer connected to your printer. Enter your Shop ID when prompted.

The print agent runs in the Windows system tray beside the clock. Open its panel, select your black & white and colour printers, then save. You can select the same printer for both.

Refresh the dashboard after the agent connects. Keep the computer and agent running while your shop accepts orders.
