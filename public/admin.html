📊 Page Range Pricing (optional)
"From this many sheets to this many = this much per sheet" — this price per-page per page, not for the entire job.
For example, with 2–5 = ₹2 a 4-page bill is 4 × 2 = ₹8. And with 6–10 = ₹1.5 an 8-page bill is 8 × 1.5 = ₹12 — the more sheets, the cheaper the rate.
Copies count too — 3 copies of 3 pages = 9 sheets, so the 6–10 range applies.
Orders outside these ranges use the standard per-page rate above. Leave the ranges empty to disable quantity pricing.
